# Blog plan

The blog lives at `/blog` with one note per post at `/blog/{slug}` and an RSS
feed at `/blog/rss`. The posts are plain data in `src/data/blog.ts`, so adding
a post is a new object in that list; no CMS and no markdown step. Each post
stays marked "Placeholder draft, not final" until the owner sets `reviewed:
true` on it after reading the final copy.

## Fifteen note topics

1. Why a card on the counter still works
2. Work your quiet day
3. Three messages worth the airtime
4. Is the reward affordable?
5. The fifteen minute visit
6. Better birthday treats
7. What a regular looks like
8. The first ten guests
9. When a guest goes quiet
10. A menu that sells the card
11. Two staff, one till
12. What to do with a full card
13. The quiet power of a thank you
14. Reading your Tuesday
15. When to stop a campaign

## Rules for every note

- Plain South African English with short words and no em dashes.
- Every claim of fact links to its source inside the paragraphs.
- No invented statistics, quotes or customer stories. If we have not measured
  it, say what we think and why, or leave it out.
- Each note ends with one action for the owner, linked to the real page
  (find, contact or the notes list itself).
- Guests are never quoted with names; ask totals stay aggregate counts.
