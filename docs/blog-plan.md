# Blog plan

The blog lives at `/blog` with one note per post at `/blog/{slug}` and an RSS
feed at `/blog/rss`. The posts are plain data in `src/data/blog.ts`, so adding
a post is a new object in that list; no CMS and no markdown step.

## Published notes

1. **Why a card on the counter still works** (`why-the-card-still-works`,

   why the QR card keeps that without the login wall.
2. **Work your quiet day** (`work-your-quiet-day`, 21 October 2026). The
   double stamp day pinned to the quiet weekday and the streak tool.
3. **Three messages worth the airtime** (`three-messages-worth-the-airtime`,

4. **Is the reward affordable?** How the monthly bonus stamp budget keeps a

5. **The Fifteen minute visit** What we check when we pop into a place, and

6. **Better birthday treats** Choosing between a bonus stamp and a free item

## Rules for every note

- Plain South African English with short words and no em dashes.
- Every claim of fact links to its source inside the paragraphs.
- Each note ends with one action for the owner, linked to the real page
  (find, contact or the notes list itself).
- Guests are never quoted with names; ask totals stay aggregate counts.
